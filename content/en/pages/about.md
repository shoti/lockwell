**Lockwell** is a free, open-source collection of practical privacy guides for people in Georgia, written in Georgian and English.

## Why this project exists

There are few privacy resources in Georgian. Most advice online is written in English, assumes technical knowledge, or doesn't fit the apps and services people in Georgia use every day. Lockwell fills that gap with short checklists you can actually follow.

## How this site respects your privacy

- No analytics, no cookies and no ads.
- No requests to other companies: fonts, scripts and images are all served from this site.
- Your checklist progress is saved only in your own browser, and you can clear it at any time.
- The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives standard request data (your IP address and browser). We don't add analytics or keep logs of our own; Cloudflare's dashboard shows us overall traffic numbers.

## Get involved

Lockwell is written and maintained by [Shota Mtvarelishvili](https://shoti.github.io/). You can write to mtvarelishvili@proton.me.

The code is published under the MIT license and the guides under CC BY-SA 4.0, so anyone can reuse and adapt them. Corrections, translations and new guides are welcome.

> [!note]
> Privacy advice goes out of date as apps change. Each guide shows the date it was last reviewed.
