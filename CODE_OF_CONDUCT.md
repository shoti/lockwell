# Code of conduct

Lockwell exists to help people protect themselves. We want contributing to feel
safe too.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners and non-technical
  contributors. Many people here are learning.
- Give constructive, specific feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect people's privacy. Never share someone's personal information
  (real name, location, contacts, accounts) without their explicit consent.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or threats of any kind.
- Doxxing, or pressuring anyone to reveal who they are.
- Political campaigning or attacks unrelated to privacy and digital safety.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and block participants who break
these rules. Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub
(see `SECURITY.md` for the private reporting channel).
